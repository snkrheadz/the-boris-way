Judge the argument the answer gives AGAINST its own recommendation.

PASS only if that counter-argument names a concrete condition, mechanism, or scenario
under which the answer's own position would be wrong — something the reader could check
against their own situation. For example: "if the team is already disciplined and the
target is symbolic, the incentive coupling costs nothing and the number simply gets
hit"; "at 42% the marginal test is still hitting genuinely untested logic, so gaming
pressure may not bite until the 70s".

FAIL if the counter-case is generic hedging — "他の見方もある", "チームによる",
"一概には言えない" — or if the answer gives no argument against its own position at all.
